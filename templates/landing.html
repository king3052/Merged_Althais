How Althais works

Say the visit.
We code it and check it.

Tell it what happened in the room — spoken or typed — and Althais does the documentation, the coding and the checking, then puts a finished claim in front of you.

Step one

You tell it about the visit.

Dictate it or type it, however you already work. Althais structures it into a full SOAP note — chief complaint, assessment, plan, duration — instead of leaving you a free-text box to fix later.

Step two

See the codes, with confidence.

Every ICD-10 and CPT suggestion comes with a confidence score, so you see at a glance what is solid and what deserves a second look. Accept, reject, or add your own.

Step three

It is checked before it leaves.

Each claim is compared against the CMS procedure-to-procedure edit table, so bundling errors are caught before a denial, not weeks after the fact.

Step four

You review. Then it is tracked.

Nothing is submitted without a person approving it. After that, every claim is followed from submission to payment, and denials surface with an appeal letter already drafted.
