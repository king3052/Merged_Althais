How It Works

Four stages, fully automated.

Capture

The provider writes the note. No templates to fight, no coding cheat sheets — Althais reads it exactly as written.

Code & Review

AI generates ICD-10 and CPT codes with a confidence score per code. The provider accepts, edits, or rejects each one.

Submit & Check

The claim is assembled, checked against payer-specific rules, and transmitted through your existing clearinghouse.

Get Paid

Status flows back in real time — submitted, accepted, paid — and remittance posts straight to your dashboard.
