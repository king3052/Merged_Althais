Roles & Permissions

The practice's access policy for each staff role.

Each role decides what people in it can open in Althais. Staff you activate through Onboarding get exactly the access their role has here, and it changes as soon as you change the role.
Only roles with Staff Records (Everyone’s) and clinic admins can see other staff members. Everyone else sees only their own profile, by clicking their name in the top bar.
A role with nothing checked gives the Staff Portal only. People added through Manager keep the login access level set there (Viewer, Provider, Biller or Admin).
